# V0 Scope

## Goal

Build a premium homepage concept that demonstrates the visual direction of the future Eline Plast website.

This is **not** the production version.

The objective is to impress the client and validate the overall design direction.

---

## What is allowed

- Placeholder text
- Placeholder statistics
- Placeholder product categories
- Placeholder gallery
- Placeholder testimonials
- Stock imagery
- Modern animations
- Reusable components

---

## What is NOT required

- Backend
- CMS
- Authentication
- Dashboard
- Final SEO optimization
- Analytics
- Contact form integration
- Real products
- Real testimonials
- Real company history

---

## Success Criteria

The client should immediately understand:

- The company positioning
- The visual identity
- The professionalism
- The premium feel
- The navigation
- The future website potential

---

## Design Direction

Premium

Minimal

Modern

Industrial

Agriculture

Technology

Nature

Water

---

## Content Strategy

Every placeholder should be easy to replace later.

Never hardcode business logic.

Everything should be reusable.

---

## Technical Goal

Build reusable components first.

Assemble the homepage from those components.

Future pages should reuse the same design system.

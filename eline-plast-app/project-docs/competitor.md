# Competitor Analysis - Irrigation Pipe Industry

## Purpose

This document helps Claude understand the competitive landscape of Eline Plast.

Eline Plast is not a general plastic manufacturer.

The company specializes in:

* Agricultural irrigation pipes
* Drip irrigation solutions
* PEHD / PEBD pipes
* Irrigation accessories
* Hydraulic agricultural solutions

Claude must analyze competitors to create a website that is:

* More professional
* More trustworthy
* More modern
* More conversion-focused

The objective is not to copy competitors but to identify opportunities.

---

Website:
https://www.bestoplast.com.tn/

Bestoplast is one of the strongest references in Tunisia for PEHD and PVC piping solutions.

* PEHD irrigation pipes

Source:
